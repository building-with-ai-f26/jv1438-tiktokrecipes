Privacy

Fridge to Feast is a student project. Last updated November 22, 2026.

What the app saves

  • Your kitchen items, pantry staples, meal plan, grocery list, recipe ideas and diet settings.
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address and name from Google, used only to know which saved data is yours.

Where it's saved

  • Not signed in: only in your own browser. Nothing is kept on our server.
  • Signed in: also in a database at Supabase (US East). Database rules let each account read and change only its own data. Nobody else using the app can see it.

Who else receives some of it

  • OpenRouter (AI): when you ask for recipe ideas, import a recipe or build a grocery list, the kitchen items, recipe text or screenshot needed for that request are sent to an AI model through OpenRouter. Your email is not sent.
  • PostHog (analytics): records page views, clicks and session replays to help improve the app.
  • Google: handles sign-in. We only receive your email and name.

We don't sell your data or use it for ads.

Deleting your data

  • From this browser: sign out, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • From your account: open an issue on the project's GitHub page asking for your account to be deleted. Don't include personal details in the issue; we'll reply there to confirm.
